Analog Function Generator and PCB

A multi stage op-amp generator taken from simulation to a hand soldered board, then measured against the model.

Role
Simulation, schematic, layout, assembly, test
Simulator
Micro-Cap 12
Amplifiers
TL082
Outputs
Square, triangle, sine
Frequency
4.4 kHz
Amplitude
10 V

Topology

Three waveforms out of one oscillator core.

  • A TL082 oscillator core produces the square wave.
  • An integrator stage turns it into a triangle.
  • Further shaping and amplification produce the sine.
  • Chaining the stages locks all three outputs to the same frequency by construction.

Simulate first

Designed and tuned in Micro-Cap 12 to 4.4 kHz at 10 V before any copper was ordered. Simulation is cheap; a board revision is not.

Board and bring up

  • A custom PCB routed in Altium Designer.
  • Through hole components populated and hand soldered.
  • Outputs captured on an oscilloscope and compared against the simulated traces, the only step that proves the design rather than the model.
Result

Measured hardware matched the simulation across all three outputs at the design frequency and amplitude.
